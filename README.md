\# RootCause AI



RootCause AI is an autonomous business investigation platform.



It investigates business questions such as:



> Why did revenue decline?



The system combines:



\- Business intelligence

\- SQL analytics

\- Statistical analysis

\- Machine learning

\- Anomaly detection

\- Forecasting

\- Root-cause analysis

\- Agentic AI

\- Evidence-based explanations
